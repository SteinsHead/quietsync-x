# Chrome Web Store listing (English)

## Name

QuietSync for X

## Summary

Review remote mute lists locally, then sync approved terms to X's native Muted words.

## Description

Public blocklists are useful, and X already has native Muted words. Keeping the two in sync should not be a repetitive manual job.

QuietSync fetches the lists you choose, then cleans, deduplicates, and classifies their terms locally in your browser. You stay in control: review the result, turn off anything too broad, and sync only the terms you approve. Because the terms are saved to X itself, they can also take effect on your phone, tablet, and other computers.

With QuietSync you can:

- subscribe to public lists or add your own HTTPS TXT and JSON sources;
- normalize whitespace, casing, duplicates, and common invisible characters;
- review terms by categories such as scams, spam, engagement bait, crypto, adult content, harassment, politics, and AI slop;
- add new terms without automatically removing existing X settings;
- choose timeline, notification, audience, and duration options before syncing;
- export a local backup or stop a sync at any time.

Automatic syncing is off by default. A normal sync starts only after you select terms and confirm the action.

### Privacy

QuietSync has no account system, ads, analytics, or telemetry. Source URLs, terms, review choices, and sync history are stored locally in your browser. Dictionary requests do not include cookies, X account data, or a referring page.

On X, QuietSync runs only on the Muted words settings route and uses the visible form. It does not read passwords, cookies, or browsing history, and it does not call a private X API.

As with any normal web request, a remote dictionary host can still see connection information such as your IP address and user agent. Exported backups can contain private terms and source URLs, so review them before sharing.

### Please note

- QuietSync is an independent project and is not affiliated with X Corp.
- X Muted words mainly affect the Home timeline and notifications, not search results.
- A future X interface change may temporarily interrupt form automation. QuietSync stops instead of guessing when the expected controls are missing.
- For a large list, start with a small batch of 20–50 terms.
